About Me

I am an Automation undergraduate at Hangzhou Dianzi University. My work connects visual perception, structured representation learning and intelligent decision-making, with projects in medical imaging, UAV inspection, radar sensing, agricultural diagnosis and robot learning.

I lead two national undergraduate innovation training projects and have served as the vision lead of a national first-prize smart car team. I am interested in building models and systems whose evidence, robustness and deployment costs can be examined together.

I am always open to research discussions and collaboration. Please feel free to contact me at 24061733 [at] hdu.edu.cn.
