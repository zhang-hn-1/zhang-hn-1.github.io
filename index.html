01Research & projects

L-RDTNet: Low-altitude radar target recognition

R.01

Advisor: Assoc. Prof. Haicheng Tu · Radar perception / Edge intelligence

Investigating radar target recognition with structured range–Doppler–time (R-D-T) representations, focusing on weak echoes, clutter and similar motion patterns. Conducted comparisons and ablations against handcrafted features, CNNs, 3D CNNs and Transformer-based baselines, with inference evaluation on Jetson.

Project evaluation: 95.06% accuracy · 95.04% Macro-F1 · 8.37M parameters · 103.41 FPS on Jetson

RadarR-D-TPyTorchJetson

Multimodal learning for scleral images and carotid plaque

R.02

Advisor: Prof. Jiuwen Cao · Medical image analysis

Exploring non-invasive scleral imaging for carotid plaque risk prediction. My work covers data preprocessing and model architecture improvements, with ablation studies to evaluate model components and investigate potential associations between ocular features and carotid plaque.

Multimodal learningMedical imagingPyTorch

Spherical robot navigation with PPO

R.03

Research internship project at Zhejiang University · Advisor: Prof. You Wang

Built parallel physics simulations in Isaac Gym, including spherical dynamics, torque control and a point-to-point closed-loop navigation baseline. Designed observations, rewards, success criteria and independent resets, then ran initial end-to-end PPO training with an MLP policy to validate the training and inference pipeline.

Simulation setup: 4 parallel environments × 27 spheres per environment

Isaac GymPPOReinforcement learning

Smart car: Embedded vision and autonomous control

R.04

Vision lead · Advisor: Shanen Yu

Designed and deployed an embedded vision perception system for high-speed autonomous driving, contributing to complex track recognition and vehicle control. The team received a National First Prize in the National University Student Smart Car Competition.
