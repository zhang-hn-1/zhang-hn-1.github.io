Accepted papers

AcceptedFirst author

Robust and Lightweight YOLO12-Based Framework for Small Vehicle Detection

Hangning Zhang, Yingjun Guan, Haicheng Tu

ICIBA 2026

A UAV vehicle detector that combines robust small-object localization with targeted backbone simplification. The study focuses on the trade-off between localization stability and deployment cost in aerial imagery.

Method & study details

Introduces Normalized Wasserstein Distance (NWD) into bounding-box regression and replaces the deepest A2C2f block with C3Ghost. Experiments on EVD4UAV compare detection quality, parameter count, computation and inference latency. Accepted by the 2026 IEEE 5th International Conference on Information Technology, Big Data and Artificial Intelligence.

AcceptedThird author

Weakly Supervised Privacy-Aware Load Profile Matching for Flexible Resource Screening in Smart Grids

Mingyue Zhang, Haicheng Tu, Hangning Zhang, Yibo Zhu, Xinghua Zhou

ITOEC 2026

PA-LPF combines perturbed load-shape embeddings with compact daily aggregate descriptors to support privacy-aware screening of flexible demand-side resources.

Method & study details

Under a strict no-label-rule validation protocol, the manuscript reports approximately 0.927 AUC and 0.812 F1. Ablation and robustness experiments examine privacy budgets, temporal masking and cross-group evaluation. Accepted by the 2026 IEEE 9th Information Technology and Mechatronics Engineering Conference.

SubmittedSecond author

CAS-DC: Evidence-Grounded Multimodal Multi-Agent Diagnosis and Safe Report Generation for Tomato Leaf Disease

PRCV

An evidence-grounded workflow that turns images and optional field context into structured symptoms, retrieved evidence, differential reasoning and safety-reviewed diagnostic reports.

Method & study details

Combines DINOv3-based classification and segmentation, Qwen3-VL symptom extraction, knowledge/case/counter-case retrieval and shared-blackboard collaboration. The manuscript reports 97.65% classification accuracy and 97.83% macro-F1; these classification metrics are distinct from the safety evaluation of generated reports.

SubmittedFirst author

Context-Preserved Attention-Refined Global–Local Learning for Ocular Surface Image-Based Carotid Plaque Screening

Hangning Zhang, Dinghan Hu, Shichen Zhang, Le Luo, Jiuwen Cao

Computerized Medical Imaging and Graphics (CMIG)

ARGL-Net explores ocular surface cues associated with ultrasound-defined carotid plaque through complementary global context, local detail and attention-refined feature fusion.

Method & study details

The study uses 44,375 left-eye images and subject-level evaluation. A ResNet-based global branch, a lightweight detail branch, attention refinement and gated fusion yield a reported AUC of 80.74%, Macro-F1 of 78.50% and accuracy of 80.00%. Comparative, ablation, perturbation and visualization analyses support the study; external validation is still needed before clinical use.

SubmittedFirst author

Edge-Deployable Range-Doppler-Time Learning for Industrial Low-Altitude Radar Target Recognition

Measurement

L-RDTNet learns radar-specific spatial and temporal representations from range–Doppler sequences for low-altitude recognition under clutter, weak returns and signal degradation.

Method & study details

The model progressively encodes coarse energy responses, local range–Doppler textures, short-term motion and compact target-level features. The manuscript compares conventional methods, CNNs, 3D CNNs and Transformers, tests SNR degradation and weak-target attenuation, and evaluates clip-level inference on RK3588.

Chronology-Constrained Reference-Guided Reconstruction of Stealthily Corrupted Smart-Meter Measurements

Mingyue Zhang, Haicheng Tu, Hangning Zhang, Zhiyuan Hu, Yuyuan Li, Yongxiang Xia, Xi Chen

TREL

FC-PAR connects interval-level corruption localization with selective reconstruction, aiming to restore usable measurements while preserving unaffected intervals.

Method & study details

Constructs mean, history-attention and similar-day references only from eligible preceding household records. Localization probabilities gate additive corrections; a preservation objective controls clean-measurement drift. Evaluation on Low Carbon London considers localization, reconstruction and recovery of downstream forecasting without oracle masks or future information.

SubmittedFirst author

AnchorIR: Degradation-Aware Clean-Source Anchoring for Lightweight UAV Image Restoration

Hangning Zhang, Haicheng Tu, Mingyue Zhang

JMI

A lightweight all-in-one restoration network that evaluates both image reconstruction fidelity and whether a restored UAV observation retrieves its correct clean reference.

Method & study details

Combines training-only degradation classification and frozen-verifier anchoring, retaining a 2.55M-parameter backbone at inference. Across seven PLAMD degradation types, the manuscript reports 28.79 dB mean PSNR and 69.81% Top-1 retrieval. Source-level analysis finds opposing changes in fidelity and retrieval rank for 30.9% of test sources, motivating evaluation beyond reconstruction averages.
