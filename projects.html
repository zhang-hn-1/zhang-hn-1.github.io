Radar perception & edge deployment

L-RDTNet · Measurement submission

Develop radar-specific representations rather than treating every range–Doppler frame as an independent natural image. Investigate clutter, similar motion patterns and weak returns through structured R-D-T learning.

Approach & work

Work spans representation design, conventional and deep-learning baselines, ablations, degradation tests and edge inference. The current manuscript evaluates RK3588 clip-level deployment; platform-specific measurements are kept separate from earlier prototypes.

Privacy-aware and reliable smart-meter analytics

PA-LPF / FC-PAR · collaborative research

Study two complementary problems: screening flexible demand-side resources from privacy-perturbed profiles, and recovering measurement records after stealthy corruption.

Approach & work

PA-LPF fuses perturbed embeddings with daily aggregate descriptors. FC-PAR uses chronology-constrained references and localization-guided repair. Evaluation considers privacy–utility, temporal boundaries and downstream forecast recovery.

Spherical robot simulation & PPO navigation

Zhejiang University research internship project · advisor: You Wang

Build torque-driven spherical dynamics and a point-to-point closed-loop navigation baseline in Isaac Gym, supporting four parallel environments with 27 spheres per environment.

Approach & work

Implement observations, rewards, success criteria and independent resets. Complete initial end-to-end PPO training with an MLP policy and connect training to inference as a baseline for subsequent policy comparisons.

High-speed embedded vision & vehicle control

Vision lead · national and provincial first prizes

Approach & work

Connect visual deviation to steering through a PID interface, with look-ahead strategies for corner deceleration and straight-line acceleration. The team received a National First Prize in the smart car competition.
